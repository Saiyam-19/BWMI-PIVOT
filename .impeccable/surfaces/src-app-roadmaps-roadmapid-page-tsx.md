# Generated Roadmap Workspace

## Mode

Operate.

## Audience

An Indian citizen, founder, small-business owner, exporter, importer, bidder, or family member who knows the outcome they need but not the responsible authorities, dependencies, portals, and proofs.

## Job

Understand the complete consequence graph for one outcome, identify the next safe task, and move through verified government or regulated journeys without invented certainty.

## Task

Scan the dependency structure, switch to the accessible linear order when useful, open a task, follow its verified portal or offline steps, resolve blockers, and advance state only when the required evidence permits it.

## Proof

The user can identify the expected completion proof, explicitly confirm receiving it, see the task become completed, and continue along newly ready dependencies.

## Constraints

- Preserve every known dependency and keep missing facts or unsupported evidence visibly fail-closed.
- Keep official links and important instructions usable without pan, zoom, hover, or drag; retain the complete linear view.
- Maintain visible focus, screen-reader state labels, reduced motion, and 44px touch targets on mobile.
- Open official portals in a new browser context; the product guides but does not submit, hold credentials, or automate government sites.
- Keep personal answers and proof confirmations private to the local roadmap and out of shared output.
- Keep the surrounding shell purpose-built for Indian government service. The pinned reference governs this generated workspace only.

## Chosen Direction

Use the shipped Operate-mode composition: the navy government-service header leads into a concise white roadmap summary, truthful progress, and immediate canvas/linear tabs. The canvas is a tall centered document plane with a dominant vertical dependency spine, generous branches, compact black-outlined state nodes, blue connectors, a compact legend, and restrained pan/zoom controls. The grammar follows the pinned roadmap.sh Software Architect reference without adopting its brand, copy, community, account, or advertising patterns.

## Memorable Interaction

The central dependency spine is the index into an evidence sheet: selecting any compact task node opens a substantial right-side journey document that explains actionability, authority, prerequisites, exact verified portal or offline steps, blockers, sources, and expected proof. Confirming the proof updates the roadmap and reveals newly ready dependencies; selecting a withheld node instead opens the same sheet with a conspicuous stop rather than fabricated instructions.
